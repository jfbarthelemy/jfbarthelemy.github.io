MicroPoroChemoMechanics

MicroPoroChemoMechanics is a GitHub organization of which I am one of the owners. It gathers a Julia ecosystem for the coupled micro-chemo-mechanics of porous reactive media, from molecular-level thermodynamic equilibrium up to the macroscopic poromechanical response. The five packages are registered in Julia’s General registry and install with a plain Pkg.add.

TensND.jl

Julia package for symbolic and numerical tensor calculations of any order and dimension in arbitrary coordinate systems, with structured tensor types carrying built-in symmetries.

ChemistryLab.jl
Julia
Chemistry
Materials Science

A Julia computational chemistry toolkit — formula handling, species management, stoichiometric matrices, thermodynamic databases and chemical equilibrium. Initially aimed at low-carbon cementitious materials and aqueous solutions.

Backend packages

OptimaSolver.jl
Julia
Optimization
Thermodynamics

Julia port of Allan Leal’s Optima C++ library: a primal-dual interior-point solver for Gibbs energy minimization, with diagonal Hessian structure and implicit-differentiation sensitivity matrices. The equilibrium backend of ChemistryLab.jl.

DECUHR.jl
Julia
Quadrature
Numerical Analysis

Pure-Julia port of the DECUHR algorithm (Espelid & Genz, 1994) for adaptive integration of functions with vertex singularities over hyper-rectangles, pluggable into Integrals.jl.
